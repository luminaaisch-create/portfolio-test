About

I’m a designer who writes production code, based in Stockholm.

For eight years I’ve worked between design and engineering — sketching in Figma in the morning and shipping the same idea in the browser by afternoon. Working in both means fewer handoffs and fewer surprises.

Outside work I teach an evening course on accessible web design and spend too long choosing typefaces.
